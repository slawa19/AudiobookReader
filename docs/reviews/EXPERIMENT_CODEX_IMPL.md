tasks and accepts the work, the external review of a batch is done by Fable 5.2. The purpose of the log is to give the
owner data for evaluating the approach. It is not a source of truth about the code.

## Conditions

- Executor: `codex exec -m gpt-6.1-sol -c model_reasoning_effort="high" --sandbox danger-full-access`.
  The requested `gpt-6.2-sol` is unavailable: "model is not supported when using Codex with a ChatGPT account".
- No sandbox: in `workspace-write` Codex does not see the WSL distribution (`WSL_E_DISTRO_NOT_FOUND`) and cannot run
  `scripts/test.sh`. Verified by trial runs on 2026-11-15.
- Each agent works in its own worktree `D:\Temp\claude\wt-<slug>` on its own branch; it runs the tests itself.
- Acceptance by the orchestrator: a full run in the main worktree after the merge, checking the report's claims against
  the code.

## Interim observations (stage 1, 2026-11-15)

- Nine executor tasks (twelve launches), about 2.35 million Codex tokens and 4 hours 40 minutes of total agent time;
  they worked two or three in parallel. Plus 335 thousand tokens of the two Fable reviewers.
- Eight tasks of nine — with narrow boundaries (environment, parsers, storage, file intake, pagination check, the
  composition root, two sets of fixes) were delivered from the first round and accepted without rework. The executors'
  reports in these tasks proved accurate: the gaps ("the screen is not moved", "Kotlin is not verified without the
  phone") were named by the agents themselves.
- The executor's green tests did not mean correctness: the external review found 21 groups of defects in the accepted
  code, almost all confirmed by the reviewers' tests. The executors' samples were "correct" files.
- The only task with rework is the reader screen: a large existing file, behavior spread over many tests, and a brief
  that left an architectural choice to the agent. The only case of fitting the tests is there too.
- The orchestrator read line by line only the Kotlin and the reader screen test diff; the rest was accepted by reports,
  the run in the main worktree and the external review.
- On the owner's real books and on the phone, stage 1 is not verified (except installing the APK): there are no books in
  `test/local_books/`, the phone was disconnected at delivery.

## Architecture and code quality review (2026-11-16)

Two reviewers on `3b71e04`, in parallel, by reading: Opus 5.5 — 11.2 min, 294 thousand tokens; `gpt-6-astra` — 13.5 min,
290 thousand tokens. The result is [ARCH_REVIEW_2026-11-16.md](ARCH_REVIEW_2026-11-16.md), the work is
[spec 002](../specs/002-cleanup-after-reviews.md).

- The module graph matched the declared one for both. There are no architecture violations; the weak spot is the seams
  of the parallel slices: one decision in two or three places.
- 16 findings, all checked by the orchestrator against the code; 13 accepted, 2 rejected, 1 deferred. None turned out
  false; for two the harm is a forecast. The overlap of the reviewers is 7 findings of 16: one reviewer would have found
  about half.
- The assessment of the Codex code quality: 3.5 out of 5 (Opus is stricter, Astra more generous). Strong — failure paths
  and tests; weak — accumulating flags and duplicates between slices. The duplicates are equally a consequence of
  acceptance without a pass across the slices.
- Spec 002 (launches 32–34): three narrow briefs with measured facts and lines — 27 minutes of agent work, 306 thousand
  tokens, not a single rework. This is the same conclusion as at stage 1: a narrow brief with facts is delivered from the
  first round; rework occurred only where the agent was left an architectural choice (the reader screen).
- Checking on the phone (2026-11-16) was handed to Sonnet agents by the owner's decision ("why are you spending Fable
  tokens on testing?"): three launches, 198 + 142 thousand tokens and 92 thousand for checking the fix; one defect was
  found that neither 870 tests nor five reviews had seen — the notification after the end of listening. The conclusion
  for evaluating the experiment: platform behavior (service, notification) is not caught by tests on the computer; a run
  of the checklist on the device is needed after every stage, and it is work for a cheap model.
