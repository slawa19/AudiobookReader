Demo books (dama.json, kapitanskaya.json). Built 2026-11-14 by scripts/prototype-texts.py from ru.wikisource.org.
They are used by the interface prototype and by tests; the app's own parser does not depend on them.

The texts are public-domain works (Wikisource template PD-old-70: the authors died more than 70 years ago):
  A. S. Pushkin, "Kapitanskaya dochka" (The Captain's Daughter), 1836, chapters I-III
  A. P. Chekhov, "Ionych", 1898, and "Dama s sobachkoy" (The Lady with the Dog), 1899

Source pages on Wikisource (https://ru.wikisource.org/) and the revision ids the texts were taken from. A revision
opens at https://ru.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=<id>.

    Капитанская дочка (Пушкин)/1960 (СО)/Глава III   revision 5753695
